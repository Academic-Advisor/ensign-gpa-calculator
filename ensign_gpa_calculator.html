Grading System

Grades are determined by each instructor based upon an evaluation of all assigned and completed coursework. Course participation, mastery of subject matter, and promise of continuing success in sequential courses are all criteria used to evaluate progress.

A – Outstanding: Outstanding understanding, application, and integration of subject material. Demonstrates original thinking, skillful use of concepts, and ability to solve complex problems.

B – Significant: Considerable understanding, application, and incorporation of course materials.

C – Sufficient: Sufficient understanding of subject matter and participation in the Learning Signature.

D – Poor: Poor performance and initiative to learn and apply course materials. Retaking the course or remediation may be necessary.

E – Failure: Indicates failure in the course.

P – Pass: Passing grade. Does not affect GPA.

W – Withdrawal: Withdrawal from the class. Does not affect GPA.

I – Incomplete: Conditional grade requiring approval and completion of remaining coursework.

IP – In Progress: Does not affect GPA.

T – Temporary: Temporary grade used for qualifying course disruptions.

CR – Credit: Credit given. Does not affect GPA.

NC – No Credit: No credit awarded. Does not affect GPA.

NR – Not Reported: Grade has not been reported by the instructor.

V – Audit: Course taken for audit. Does not affect GPA or SAP.

Credit Definitions

  • Attempted: Total credits in which a student has registered.
  • Earned: Total credits successfully completed.
  • Passed: Credits earned that do not factor into GPA.
  • Quality: Credits that factor into GPA.
  • Points: Total grade points earned.

Calculating GPA and SAP

GPA: Quality Points divided by Quality Credits.

SAP: Earned Credits divided by Attempted Credits.

Financial aid eligibility may use additional GPA and Satisfactory Academic Progress requirements. Consult the Ensign College Financial Aid Office for official eligibility determinations.
